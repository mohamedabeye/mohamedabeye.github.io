About me

I work where statistics, computing and public finance meet. At the Inland Revenue Department of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Development, I design and maintain the national tax databases and the statistics that support revenue management and evidence-based decisions.

My research asks how governments can forecast revenue well when data are short and economies change fast: combining economic drivers with time-series models, Bayesian methods and honest forecast ranges.

I also teach programming and data structures at the University of Hargeisa and founded Damaana, a data and software consultancy.

Research

Preprint · 2026

Forecasting Customs Revenue in a Data-Scarce Economy: Revisiting a Linear Trend Model for Kalabaydh, Somaliland

Compares the original linear trend with log-linear, Holt, ARIMA with drift and Bayesian models on 1999–2012 customs revenue. A random walk with drift cut out-of-sample forecast error from 41% to 14.6%.
